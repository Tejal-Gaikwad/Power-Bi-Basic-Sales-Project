# Power-Bi-Basic-Sales-Project

## Project Overview
Developed an interactive Sales and Profit Analysis Dashboard using Power BI to analyze category-wise profit, payment modes, customer segments, state-wise sales, and monthly trends. Used charts, KPI cards, and regional slicers to visualize data, identify business insights, monitor performance, and support data-driven decision-making.

## Project Objectives

- Analyze overall sales and order performance.
- Identify the top 15 states based on sales.
- Analyze category-wise profit distribution.
- Compare profit across different payment modes.
- Understand customer segment-wise performance.
- Track monthly sales trends.
- Explore regional sales performance.

## Key Features

1. **Total Orders Analysis**
   - Displays the total number of orders.
   - Provides an overview of overall order performance.

2. **Top 15 States by Sales**
   - Displays the top 15 states based on sales.
   - Helps identify the highest-performing states.

3. **Payment Mode and Segment-wise Profit**
   - Analyzes profit across COD, Online, and Card payment modes.
   - Compares Consumer, Corporate, and Home Office segments.

4. **Category-wise Profit Analysis**
   - Visualizes profit distribution across Technology, Office Supplies, and Furniture categories.

5. **Monthly Sales Trends**
   - Displays monthly sales performance across different customer segments.
   - Helps understand sales fluctuations over time.

6. **Regional Filtering**
   - Includes Central, South, East, and West regions.
   - Allows users to interactively filter dashboard data.

## Tools & Technologies

- Microsoft Power BI
- Power Query
- DAX (Data Analysis Expressions)
- Data Cleaning and Transformation
- Data Visualization

## Key Insights

- California has the highest sales among the displayed states.
- Technology contributes the largest share of category-wise profit.
- The dashboard enables comparison of profit across payment modes and customer segments.
- Monthly sales trends help identify changes in sales performance.
- Regional filters allow users to explore performance across four regions.
